## Honest caveats

- Pricing is a launch hypothesis, not a validated price.
- Upgrade rate (20% in month 3) is an assumption; the plan has not been tested against zero upgrades.
- Living costs covered by $2,000/mo draw are modest — most founders need more.
- Demo evidence is one decision on one app, not a typical-customer ROI.
- Taxes, substantial delivery labor, usage spikes excluded.
- The $5,000 starting cash is assumed to be self-funded; no outside funding modelled.
